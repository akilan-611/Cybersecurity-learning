TCP :
    guarantees delivery through strict, connection-oriented process.                   
    it establish reliabl connection via 3-way handshake.
  1. SYN(synchronize) : cilent send packet with SYN flag.
  2. SYN-ACK : server receive SYN, , allocates resources, and responds with both SYN and ACK flags.
  3. ACK(acknowledge) : client send final ACK packet back.
 after complete handshake data begin to flow.
Reliability Features
    Once connected, TCP ensures perfect delivery:
    Sequencing: Every segment is numbered. If segments arrive out of order, TCP reassembles them correctly.
    Acknowledgments (ACKs): The receiver must send an ACK for every chunk of data received.
    Retransmissions:sender doesn't receive ACK within a certain timeframe, it assumes packet was lost and resends it.
  defenders use stateful firewall for search SYN handshake inside network but never saw it then drop the packets.
UDP :
    opposite to TCP .no handshake .no acknomledgments ,no sequencing.it doesn't care data .keep sending.
    it  is connectionless and unreliable also fast.used in DNS queries VoIP & video call . online gaming.
  Defenders rely on TCP's predictable handshake for stateful firewall rules, while attackers abuse UDP's lack of 
  authentication for DDoS amplification and spoofing.
