The Data link layer :
         ultimate local courier.  relies on two critical components: MAC Addresses and the ARP Protocol.only cares about data not mediater.
MAC Address :(hardware fingerprint)
         IP address is logical. it can change depends on network.MAC address (Madia Access Control)is physical.A MAC address is a 48-bit number burned directly into
         firmware of your Network Interface Card (NIC) at factory. It is permanent, unchangeable fingerprint of hardware.in hexadecimal,separated by : 
         48 bits-->2 haives.
   OUI(Organizationally Unique Identifier - I 24 bits identify manufacturer.
   NIC specific : II 24 bits are unique serial num assing by manufacturer.
        defender use MAC filtering,MAC address useful for network inventory and tracking physical device locations.
     wifi understand MAC not IP.
ARP(address resolution protocol) :
         ARP is bridge between IP AND MAC.it work  in two-step process.
1) ARP request(broadcast) :
         it check local memory (ARP cache),already knows MAC .doesn't shouts out entire local network via a broadcost.
2) ARP reply(unicast) :
         every device in LAN receive broadcast.device hold IP respond.send a direct unicast reply device save this mapping in ARP cache, now data > packet > frame
         > access wire.
ARP spoofing :
         ARP stateless,blindly trust we ARP spoofing to trick device into sending traffic to attacker enabling local man-in-the-middle attack .
    defender-use DAI(dynamic ARP inspection)
